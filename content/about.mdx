# About

VoiceOverStudioAI publishes practical guides and workflows around AI voiceovers, AI video and content production: ElevenLabs for voice and dubbing, Pictory AI for turning scripts and articles into videos, captions, projects and API use cases.

## Editorial independence & affiliate links

Some links may be affiliate links. If you purchase or sign up through those links, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. This helps keep the site running and guides updated.

Affiliate relationships do not guarantee coverage and do not change our recommendations. When something is uncertain (pricing changes, feature availability), we say so and encourage you to verify on the official provider website.

Pictory AI pages use the affiliate link `https://pictory.ai?ref=voiceoverstudioai`. Pricing, quotas, AI Credits and features are checked against official sources when pages are updated.

## Updates & corrections

AI tools evolve quickly. We update key pages when we spot meaningful changes. If you notice an issue, please contact us with the page URL and the correction details.
