# Methodology

This page explains how we build our guides and how we evaluate tools and workflows.

## 1) What we focus on

- **Audio quality:** clarity, stability, noise/artifacts, consistency.
- **Pronunciation & multilingual:** language coverage, phonemes, edge cases.
- **Workflow fit:** Projects, Dubbing, voice design, speed of iteration.
- **Controls & safety:** consent, voice settings, safeguards.
- **Pricing & limits:** pay-as-you-go vs subscriptions, quotas, hidden constraints.

## 2) Sources and checks

We cross-check claims using official documentation, product pages, changelogs, and practical workflow testing where possible. See: [Sources](/sources).

## 3) Affiliate transparency

We may earn commissions via affiliate links. That does not change our editorial standards. We try to disclose affiliations and keep recommendations practical.

## 4) Updates

We update pages when features, pricing, or APIs change significantly.
