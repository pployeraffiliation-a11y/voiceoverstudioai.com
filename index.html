FAQs

How many languages does ElevenLabs support?
29+ languages with native prosody and code-switching for multilingual scripts.
Can I dub videos?
Yes. AI Dubbing imports SRT/VTT, aligns speech, and exports captions plus audio.
Is there a free plan?
Yes. A free tier lets you test TTS, Dubbing, and basic API calls before upgrading.
Is voice cloning allowed?
Only with consent. ElevenLabs adds watermarking and misuse detection to protect voices.
Is there an API?
Yes. Streaming and batch endpoints with analytics to monitor usage and quotas.
